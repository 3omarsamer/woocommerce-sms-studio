# V2 — Automated Customer Lifecycle Marketing

**Planned for November 2026. None of these automations are asserted to exist in RC18.**

1. **Win-back:** A customer with no new eligible purchase for approximately 30 days can qualify for a personalized reminder.
2. **Collection-based recommendations:** Suggest relevant products based on a previous eligible collection purchase.
3. **New order confirmations:** Transactional SMS on appropriate WooCommerce order events.
4. **Shipment tracking:** Send tracking information when a valid fulfillment/tracking reference becomes available.
5. **Post-purchase follow-ups:** Personalized outreach after an order, subject to contact preferences.
6. **Automation controls:** Trigger deduplication, idempotency, send windows, consent/opt-out, suppression, budgets, retries and audit reporting.

Marketing messages and transactional notifications require distinct eligibility/privacy rules. Design automations to prevent repeated messages, incorrect purchase assumptions and duplicate provider submissions.
